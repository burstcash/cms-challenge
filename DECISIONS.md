# Decisions

Use this file for anything you had to decide that the brief did not settle for
you. We read it before we read your code.

For each decision, we are looking for three things: what you chose, why you
chose it, and what you would have asked us if we were colleagues rather than a
brief.

Also record what you deliberately did not build. Leaving something out on
purpose and saying so is a good answer. Leaving it out silently is not.

---

**Why.** <the reasoning, including the option you rejected>

**What I would have asked.** <the question you would have sent us>
